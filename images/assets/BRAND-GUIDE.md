# Ascent Learning — Logo & brand notes

## Identity
The ascending A and open-book forms connect academic progress with learning. A warm gold accent adds a focal point. The wordmark pairs a refined serif with spaced uppercase lettering.

## Master asset
`ascent-learning-logo.png` is a 1942 × 809 pixel transparent RGBA PNG. This is the high-resolution raster master, not an editable vector file. It is suitable for the website and digital business materials, and measures approximately 6.47 × 2.70 inches at 300 pixels per inch for print placement.

## Use
- Preserve the original aspect ratio; never stretch the logo.
- Use on white or warm cream (#FCFAF5) backgrounds for strong contrast.
- Keep clear space around it of at least the height of the LEARNING lettering.
- Use the full lockup at 190 CSS pixels wide or larger so the small lettering stays readable.
- Keep the transparent master; do not use screenshots of the logo.
- For large-format signage or a printer requesting vectors, commission a faithful outlined vector production file from this master.
