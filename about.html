Meet Aarushi Agrawal

Passionate Educator | Academic Coach | Student Success Advocate

Hello! I'm Aarushi Agrawal.

I am passionate about helping children become confident, successful, and independent learners.

For 10 years, I have worked in Cupertino Union School District (CUSD), with students from kindergarten through grade 8, supporting students, teachers, administrators, and families in creating positive learning experiences. Throughout my career, I have gained valuable insight into how students learn, where they struggle, and what helps them grow academically and personally.

My experience inside schools allows me to understand classroom expectations, curriculum standards, and the challenges students face every day.

I believe tutoring should be personalized, encouraging, and focused on long term success, going beyond completing homework assignments.

My sessions are designed to:

  • Strengthen foundational skills
